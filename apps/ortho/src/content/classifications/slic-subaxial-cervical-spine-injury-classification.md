## Overview

SLIC is a point-based severity score for subaxial cervical (C3–C7) trauma that sums three components — fracture morphology, discoligamentous complex (DLC) integrity, and neurologic status — into a single operative-versus-nonoperative threshold. It is used alongside the AO Spine Subaxial Cervical Injury Classification for any subaxial cervical fracture or fracture-dislocation.

## Types

| Score component | Definition | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Morphology** | No abnormality 0; compression 1; burst +1 (=2 total); distraction (facet perch, hyperextension) 3; rotation/translation (facet dislocation, unstable teardrop/advanced flexion compression) 4 | 0–4 |
| **Discoligamentous complex (DLC)** | Intact 0; indeterminate (isolated MRI signal, interspinous widening) 1; disrupted (widening of disc space, facet perch/dislocation) 2 | 0–2 |
| **Neurologic status** | Intact 0; root injury 1; complete cord injury 2; incomplete cord injury 3; continuous cord compression with neuro deficit +1 | 0–4 |
| **Total ≤3** | Sum of the above | **Nonoperative** (collar) |
| **Total 4** | Sum of the above | **Indeterminate** — surgeon judgment |
| **Total ≥5** | Sum of the above | **Operative** |

## Pearls

- Complete DLC disruption or facet perch/dislocation with cord compression is surgical regardless of the total score — these findings override a borderline numeric total.
- Continuous cord compression with a neurologic deficit adds an extra point on top of the base neurologic-status score, reflecting that ongoing compression (not just the deficit itself) independently worsens the injury severity.
- Stable subaxial injuries (no cord, no instability) are managed with a rigid collar for 6–8 weeks; SLIC ≥5, facet fracture-dislocation, or cord compression are what push management to ACDF or posterior fixation.
- Used together with the AO Spine Subaxial Cervical Injury Classification (morphology/facet/neurologic/case-specific modifiers) rather than as a standalone replacement — the two frameworks are meant to be applied side by side.
