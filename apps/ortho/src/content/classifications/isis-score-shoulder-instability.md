## Overview

The ISIS (Instability Severity Index Score, Balg & Boileau) is a six-variable preoperative clinical/radiographic score, each item worth 0 or 1–2 points for a total of 0–10, developed to predict recurrence risk after **isolated arthroscopic Bankart repair** and to flag patients who should instead have a bone-block (Latarjet) procedure.

## Types

| Type/Grade | Definition | Treatment implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Score component: Age at surgery ≤20 years** | 2 points | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **Score component: Competitive sport participation** | 2 points | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **Score component: Contact/forced overhead sport** | 1 point | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **Score component: Shoulder hyperlaxity (clinical)** | 1 point | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **Score component: Hill-Sachs lesion visible on AP radiograph in external rotation** | 2 points | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **Score component: Glenoid loss of contour on AP radiograph** | 2 points | Contributes to overall risk stratification |
| **ISIS ≤3** | Low total score | Isolated arthroscopic Bankart repair; low recurrence (~5–10%) |
| **ISIS 4–5** | Gray zone | Individualized judgment; consider remplissage augmentation |
| **ISIS ≥6** | High total score | Primary Latarjet/bone-block recommended (isolated Bankart recurrence otherwise rises to >70% in this group) |

## Pearls

- ISIS is a risk-stratification aid, not a rigid rule — interpret it alongside direct bone-loss measurement and glenoid track (on-track/off-track) status rather than in isolation.
- A 2024 multicenter validation (Blommestein et al., *KSSTA*) confirmed ISIS remains predictive of recurrence (OR 1.55 per point) but suggested a **lower cutoff (~≥4)**, rather than the classic ≥6, may better flag recurrence risk in contemporary, heterogeneous populations — proposed cutoff bands of 0–3 / 4–6 / 7–10 are under reconsideration.
- The score was developed specifically to select patients for arthroscopic Bankart vs. open (Latarjet) stabilization — its predictive validity is best established in that decision context, not as a general instability severity measure.
