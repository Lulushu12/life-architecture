## Overview

The EBJIS (European Bone and Joint Infection Society) 2021 definition, developed jointly with MSIS and the ESCMID Study Group for Implant-Associated Infections, classifies periprosthetic joint infection (PJI) using a rule-in/rule-out algorithm rather than a single-timepoint score. It is designed to be applied at every stage of work-up — clinic referral, aspiration, and theatre — moving a patient stepwise from "unlikely" through "likely" to "confirmed" infection.

## Types

| Category | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Infection unlikely** | None of the criteria below present | Investigate alternative diagnosis (aseptic loosening, instability, adverse local tissue reaction) |
| **Infection likely** | ≥1 of: elevated CRP (unexplained by other cause); imaging suggestive of infection; new-onset synovitis or effusion with elevated synovial WBC/PMN%/leukocyte esterase below the "confirmed" threshold; single positive culture of a virulent organism | Proceed to further synovial/microbiological work-up before committing to a definitive surgical infection pathway; treat as infected if pretest probability is high and further tests support it |
| **Infected (confirmed)** | ≥1 major criterion — sinus tract communicating with the joint, visible purulence around the prosthesis, or ≥2 positive cultures with a phenotypically identical organism — OR a combination of minor criteria (elevated synovial WBC, elevated synovial PMN%, positive alpha-defensin, positive histology, single positive culture of a virulent organism plus supportive serum/synovial markers) reaching the defined threshold | Full surgical infection pathway (DAIR/one-stage/two-stage as indicated) |

## Pearls

- EBJIS is preferred by many European centers for its stepwise "unlikely → likely → confirmed" logic, which mirrors the real clinical workflow (clinic referral → aspiration → theatre) and reduces the number of patients left in a diagnostic "gray zone" compared with the fixed-threshold ICM/MSIS scoring system.
- A sinus tract or visible communication with the joint is a major criterion that confirms infection regardless of any other test — do not aspirate through it "for diagnosis"; go to theatre.
- The synovial WBC threshold EBJIS uses in the acute postoperative setting is substantially higher (~10,000 cells/µL) than the chronic threshold, because normal postoperative inflammation elevates baseline synovial WBC counts — applying the chronic threshold in the acute window risks false-positive classification.
- A head-to-head comparison of EBJIS 2021, ICM 2018, and IDSA 2013 confirmed EBJIS's stepwise framework reduces the proportion of patients left in a diagnostically uncertain category compared with fixed-threshold ICM scoring — supporting complementary rather than competing use of the two systems.
