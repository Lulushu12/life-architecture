## Overview

The Tscherne classification grades the severity of soft-tissue injury accompanying a **closed** fracture, from C0 (minimal injury) to C3 (extensive crush/degloving with established or manifest compartment syndrome). It functions as the closed-injury counterpart to the Gustilo-Anderson system for open fractures, and its grade governs whether definitive fixation can proceed immediately or must be staged until the soft-tissue envelope recovers.

## Types

| Grade | Definition | Treatment implication |
|---|---|---|
| **C0** | No/minimal soft-tissue injury, simple fracture pattern | Standard fixation, no special soft-tissue precautions |
| **C1** | Superficial abrasion or contusion from indirect injury | Watch for delayed swelling; can usually proceed with planned fixation timing |
| **C2** | Deep abrasion with local skin/muscle contusion, direct trauma, impending compartment syndrome possible | Consider staged fixation; monitor closely for evolving compartment syndrome; delay definitive plating until swelling permits |
| **C3** | Extensive skin contusion/crushing, subcutaneous degloving, established or manifest compartment syndrome, decompensated vascular injury | Damage control (external fixation) mandatory; definitive fixation delayed until soft-tissue envelope recovers (often 10–21 days) |

## Pearls

- Tscherne grading directly informs the choice between acute definitive fixation and staged/damage-control external fixation — a C2 or C3 injury forced into early definitive plating risks wound breakdown and infection.
- Because C2/C3 injuries carry impending or established compartment syndrome risk, Tscherne grade should prompt the same vigilance (serial exams, low threshold for pressure monitoring) described for acute compartment syndrome, particularly if a regional/neuraxial block is in place that could mask the cardinal pain symptom.
- The grade is a soft-tissue assessment made at presentation and should be reassessed as swelling evolves — a fracture that looks like a C1/C2 initially can progress, changing the fixation timing decision.
