## Overview

The FRI consensus definition classifies infection around orthopedic fracture-fixation implants into **confirmatory** (diagnostic on its own) and **suggestive** (raises suspicion, prompts further workup) criteria. It is used because FRI shares osteomyelitis biology but is defined specifically by the presence of an implant, which independently promotes biofilm formation and makes implant removal-versus-retention a central strategic decision.

## Types

| Criterion category | Finding | Diagnostic implication |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmatory (any one is diagnostic) | Fistula, sinus tract, or wound breakdown communicating with the bone/implant | Diagnostic — proceed to targeted treatment planning |
| Confirmatory | Purulent drainage from the wound or purulence around the implant intra-operatively | Diagnostic — proceed to targeted treatment planning |
| Confirmatory | Microorganisms isolated by culture from ≥2 separate deep tissue/implant samples (phenotypically indistinguishable), OR a single deep sample growing a highly virulent pathogen | Diagnostic — proceed to targeted treatment planning |
| Confirmatory | Histopathological confirmation of microorganisms in deep tissue with special stains | Diagnostic — proceed to targeted treatment planning |
| Suggestive (prompts further workup) | New-onset joint effusion, localized erythema, warmth, swelling, or increasing pain | Raises suspicion — pursue deep sampling/imaging |
| Suggestive | Persistent, increasing, or new wound drainage | Raises suspicion — pursue deep sampling/imaging |
| Suggestive | Radiological signs (periosteal reaction, implant loosening not explained by mechanical failure, involucrum/sequestrum) | Raises suspicion — pursue deep sampling/imaging |
| Suggestive | Unexplained persistent fever, systemic inflammatory markers elevated without another source | Raises suspicion — pursue deep sampling/imaging |

## Pearls

- Obtain ≥3–5 deep tissue samples with dedicated instruments per site — never a superficial swab.
- Send removed implants for **sonication**, which dramatically increases culture yield over tissue culture alone.
- Hold cultures 10–14 days when low-virulence organisms (*Cutibacterium acnes*, coagulase-negative staphylococci) are suspected — a single positive low-virulence culture is suggestive, not confirmatory, unless it recurs in a second sample.
- Biofilm matures over the initial 2–4 weeks post-implantation; this is the biological basis for the time-window logic behind DAIR versus staged exchange.
- An international retrospective cohort study testing the confirmatory-criteria framework against cases lacking classic confirmatory findings reported ≥1 confirmatory criterion carrying sensitivity ~97.5% and specificity ~100% for true infection, reinforcing the framework as the operational diagnostic standard.
