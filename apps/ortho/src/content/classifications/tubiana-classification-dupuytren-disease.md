## Overview

The Tubiana classification stages Dupuytren disease by total flexion contracture (MCP + PIP + DIP, in degrees) of the involved digit, from an isolated nodule with no contracture (Stage N) through severe fixed deformity (Stage IV). It is the primary driver of the choice among the three main treatment modalities — collagenase injection, percutaneous needle fasciotomy, and limited fasciectomy — and of which joint (MCP vs PIP) is involved matters as much as the stage itself.

## Types

| Stage | Total flexion contracture (MCP+PIP+DIP) | Treatment implication |
|---|---|---|
| N | Nodule only, no contracture | Observation |
| N/I | Nodule + <45° contracture | Observation vs early procedural intervention if progressive |
| I | 0-45° | Collagenase or needle fasciotomy reasonable first-line, especially for isolated MCP cord |
| II | 45-90° | Collagenase, needle fasciotomy, or limited fasciectomy depending on cord anatomy; PIP involvement favors fasciectomy for durability |
| III | 90-135° | Limited fasciectomy generally preferred (better correction, more durable for PIP disease); collagenase/fasciotomy less reliable |
| IV | >135° | Fasciectomy ± dermofasciectomy (with skin graft) for recurrent/severe disease; salvage PIP arthrodesis or amputation for fixed, longstanding PIP contracture with NV compromise |

## Pearls

- The positive tabletop test (unable to place the palm flat on a table) is the classic clinical trigger for surgical/procedural intervention, used alongside goniometric measurement of contracture per joint.
- PIP involvement, not just total contracture degree, is the key modifier: PIP cords favor limited fasciectomy for durability, while isolated MCP cords respond well to collagenase or needle fasciotomy even at lower stages.
- Diathesis markers — bilateral disease, family history, early onset (<50y), and ectopic disease (Garrod's pads, Ledderhose plantar fibromatosis, Peyronie penile fibromatosis) — predict recurrence and should be documented at initial evaluation regardless of stage.
- Collagenase Clostridium histolyticum (CCH) is best suited to MCP joint cords with contracture in the moderate range (Tubiana I-II) and is less predictable for PIP cords.
- All three modalities carry substantial recurrence risk by 5 years; the rate hierarchy is fasciectomy lowest, collagenase/needle fasciotomy higher (many series report >50% recurrence of ≥20° by 3-5 years for collagenase, and commonly >50-85% in some needle fasciotomy series, though functional retreatment thresholds matter more than radiographic/clinical recurrence alone).
- Incomplete correction of longstanding PIP contracture can occur regardless of technique, because joint capsule/collateral ligament contracture may not fully release even with adequate cord division.
- Multiple 2024-2025 RCTs and network meta-analyses converge on a shared decision-making framework: limited fasciectomy for PIP-predominant or recurrent disease and patients prioritizing durability; collagenase/needle fasciotomy for MCP-predominant or multi-ray disease in patients prioritizing rapid recovery over durability.
