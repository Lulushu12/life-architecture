## Overview

TLICS is a point-based severity score for thoracolumbar trauma that sums three components — fracture morphology, posterior ligamentous complex (PLC) integrity, and neurologic status — into a single operative-versus-nonoperative threshold. It is used alongside the AO Spine Thoracolumbar Injury Classification whenever the morphology grade alone (particularly A3/A4 burst fractures) leaves the treatment decision ambiguous.

## Types

| Score component | Definition | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Morphology** | Compression 1; burst 2; translation/rotation 3; distraction 4 | 1–4 |
| **Posterior ligamentous complex (PLC)** | Intact 0; indeterminate (MRI signal only) 2; disrupted 3 | 0–3 |
| **Neurologic status** | Intact 0; nerve root injury 2; complete cord/conus injury 2; incomplete cord/conus injury 3; cauda equina 3 | 0–3 |
| **Total ≤3** | Sum of the above | **Nonoperative** (brace) |
| **Total 4** | Sum of the above | **Surgeon/patient-specific judgment** — either operative or nonoperative reasonable |
| **Total ≥5** | Sum of the above | **Operative** |

## Pearls

- Any PLC disruption or neurologic deficit adds points and pushes the score toward surgery — these are the two components most likely to convert a borderline morphology into an operative total.
- The 4-point "gray zone" is where TLICS is weakest: 2023–2024 AO Spine treatment-algorithm validation studies show only moderate concordance between the score's recommendation and actual expert practice at this threshold, reinforcing that TLICS is a decision aid rather than a mandate.
- Indeterminate PLC status on MRI (isolated signal change without confirmed instability, scored as 2) is a common source of scoring ambiguity — this is exactly the scenario where MRI is indicated when TLICS/AO scoring is borderline on CT alone.
- Minimally invasive percutaneous pedicle screw fixation is increasingly used for TLICS 4–5 injuries not requiring direct decompression, reducing blood loss and muscle injury versus open approaches with comparable fusion/alignment outcomes.
