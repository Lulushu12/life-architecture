## Overview

The 2018 International Consensus Meeting / Musculoskeletal Infection Society (ICM/MSIS) criteria define periprosthetic joint infection (PJI) using two major criteria (either diagnostic alone) plus a weighted minor-criteria scoring system applied preoperatively and, when inconclusive, combined with intraoperative findings. It is the basis for most published outcome registries and the default framework most journals require for study eligibility.

## Types

**Diagnostic categories**

| Category | Definition | Management implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Major criterion (either alone is diagnostic)** | Two positive periprosthetic cultures with phenotypically identical organisms, OR a sinus tract communicating with the joint | Treat as PJI; no further scoring needed |
| **Infected** | Preoperative score ≥6, or combined (pre- + intraoperative) score ≥6 | Treat as PJI |
| **Inconclusive** | Preoperative score 2–5 (proceed to intraoperative criteria); combined score 4–5 | Use intraoperative findings (purulence, frozen section, single positive culture, NGS) and clinical judgment to reclassify |
| **Not infected** | Preoperative score 0–1; combined score ≤3 | Treat as aseptic failure |

**Preoperative minor criteria (weighted points)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Elevated CRP (≥10 mg/L) or D-dimer | 2 |
| Elevated ESR (≥30 mm/h) | 1 |
| Elevated synovial WBC (≥3,000 cells/µL) or leukocyte esterase strip ++ | 3 |
| Positive alpha-defensin | 3 |
| Elevated synovial PMN% (≥80%) | 2 |
| Elevated synovial CRP | 1 |

**Intraoperative criteria (added to preoperative subtotal when inconclusive)**

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Positive histology (≥5 PMN/HPF in 5 fields) | 3 |
| Positive single culture | 2 |
| Purulence | 3 |

## Pearls

- The numeric scoring system is well suited to structured, reproducible decision-making and is what most journals now require for study eligibility, making it the default framework for evidence appraisal.
- **ICM 2025** revisited and updated roughly 37 of the 2018 recommendations (21.5% of comparable questions changed), including strengthening the case for a **minimum 12-week total antibiotic course after debridement** and recognizing increased revision-arthroplasty infection risk after prior ipsilateral arthroscopy — treat ICM 2018 as the diagnostic scaffold and ICM 2025 as the current management-recommendation layer on top of it.
- Both CRP and ESR are physiologically elevated in the acute postoperative window (<6 weeks) from surgery itself; use the trend (failure to fall, or a secondary rise after an initial decline) rather than an absolute threshold in that window.
- A positive alpha-defensin is not a replacement for culture — it does not identify the organism or guide antibiotic selection, and can be falsely positive with certain metal debris or crystalline synovitis.
- Frozen section (≥5 PMN/HPF averaged across 5 fields) is most useful when preoperative work-up is inconclusive and a same-anesthetic retain-vs-remove decision is needed.
