## Overview

The Pirani score is a six-sign clinical severity scale for congenital clubfoot (CTEV), scored weekly during Ponseti casting to track correction progress and to flag feet at higher risk of needing more casts or a percutaneous Achilles tenotomy.

## Types

Six binary/half-point signs (0 = normal, 0.5 = mild, 1 = severe), split into a hindfoot contracture score (HFCS) and a midfoot contracture score (MFCS), each 0–3.

| Component | Definition | Treatment implication |
|---|---|---|
| Hindfoot contracture score (HFCS) — posterior crease | Depth/quality of the posterior ankle crease | Contributes to HFCS 0–3; tracks equinus correction |
| HFCS — empty heel | Non-palpable calcaneal tuberosity | Contributes to HFCS; persistent emptiness suggests residual hindfoot elevation |
| HFCS — rigidity of equinus | Resistance to passive dorsiflexion | Contributes to HFCS; guides readiness for tenotomy |
| Midfoot contracture score (MFCS) — curvature of lateral border of foot | Degree of lateral border curvature | Contributes to MFCS 0–3; tracks midfoot adductus correction |
| MFCS — severity of medial crease | Depth/quality of the medial midfoot crease | Contributes to MFCS |
| MFCS — lateral head of talus coverage | Palpability of the talar head laterally | Contributes to MFCS; reflects navicular reduction on the talar head |

| Total score | Management implication |
|---|---|
| 0–6 (HFCS 0–3 + MFCS 0–3) | Scored weekly during casting to track correction |
| Higher **initial** score (particularly >4–5) | Predicts a greater number of casts required and a higher likelihood of needing percutaneous Achilles tenotomy |

## Pearls

- Used serially — the trend across casts matters as much as any single score.
- A plateauing or worsening score despite technically correct casting should prompt reassessment of casting technique or suspicion of an atypical/complex foot rather than simply continuing the same approach.
- Pirani ≥5 predicts more casts and a higher tenotomy rate — a useful counseling benchmark at initial presentation.
