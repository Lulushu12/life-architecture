## Overview

The AO/OTA 23 system classifies distal radius fractures hierarchically by articular involvement (A = extra-articular, B = partial articular, C = complete articular), with each group further divided into subgroups by pattern and comminution. It is the universal, coding/research-oriented classification — most widely used in trials and structured operative documentation — though day-to-day surgical decision-making at the bedside more often draws on mechanism-based systems such as Fernandez.

## Types

| Type | Description | Treatment implication |
|---|---|---|
| **23-A** (extra-articular) | A1: ulna fractured, radius intact; A2: radius extra-articular, simple/impacted; A3: radius extra-articular, multifragmentary | Usually amenable to closed reduction/casting if reducible to acceptable alignment; K-wire or volar locking plate (VLP) if unstable/redisplaces, especially A3 |
| **23-B** (partial articular) | B1: sagittal split (radial styloid); B2: dorsal rim (Barton); B3: volar rim (volar Barton/reverse Barton) | Inherently unstable due to shear mechanism — nearly always operative (ORIF with buttress/volar locking plate); B3 volar Barton is the classic VLP indication |
| **23-C** (complete articular) | C1: articular simple + metaphyseal simple; C2: articular simple + metaphyseal multifragmentary; C3: articular multifragmentary (comminuted, e.g. die-punch) | C1/C2 generally ORIF VLP ± dorsal augmentation; C3 often needs fragment-specific fixation, dorsal plating, external fixation as adjunct, or combined approaches; highest risk of post-traumatic arthritis |

## Pearls

- The current AO/OTA classification also carries **universal qualifiers** (e.g., displacement, comminution, articular impaction, associated ulnar fracture) that further refine the A/B/C group — useful for structured operative note documentation and outcome research, though clinical decision-making usually still centers on the A/B/C/subgroup level.
- Reach for AO/OTA 23 specifically for coding, administrative purposes, and research comparability — it is universal and hierarchical, and the most widely used system in trials.
- Post-traumatic osteoarthritis correlates strongly with residual articular incongruity (>2 mm step/gap), the principal justification for anatomic articular reduction in 23-B/C patterns.
